# Code of Conduct / 行为准则

## English

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct.

We are committed to providing a welcoming, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

**Expected behavior:** be respectful and constructive, accept feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the community.

**Unacceptable behavior:** harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, publishing others' private information without permission, and any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

**Enforcement:** instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers privately through a [GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/misselvexu/grokbot-x-ops-skill/security/advisories/new) (select it for conduct reports as well) or by contacting the maintainer via their GitHub profile. All reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors who violate this code.

The full text of the Contributor Covenant 2.1, including enforcement guidelines, is available at <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.

## 简体中文

本项目采用 [贡献者公约（Contributor Covenant）2.1 版](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/zh-cn/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) 作为行为准则。

我们致力于为每一个人提供友好、无骚扰的参与环境，无论其年龄、体型、可见或不可见的残障、民族、性征、性别认同与表达、经验水平、教育程度、社会经济地位、国籍、外貌、种族、种姓、肤色、宗教信仰、性身份或性取向如何。

**期望的行为：** 尊重他人、建设性沟通、虚心接受反馈，以社区整体利益为重。

**不可接受的行为：** 骚扰、侮辱、人身或政治攻击、未经许可公开他人隐私信息，以及任何在专业场合不恰当的行为。

**执行：** 如遇不可接受的行为，可通过 [GitHub 安全公告](https://github.com/misselvexu/grokbot-x-ops-skill/security/advisories/new)（行为问题也可使用此渠道）或通过维护者的 GitHub 主页私下联系维护者。所有报告都会被及时审阅并保密。维护者有权删除、编辑或拒绝违反本准则的贡献，并可暂时或永久禁止违规者参与。

贡献者公约 2.1 版全文及执行指南见 <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/zh-cn/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>。
